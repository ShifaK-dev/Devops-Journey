# Day 21 - Resume Building for DevOps

## Objective

Create a professional one-page resume targeted towards DevOps and Cloud roles.

---

# Why Resume Matters

A resume is the first impression recruiters get.

A good resume should:

- Be clear and concise
- Highlight technical skills
- Showcase projects
- Be ATS-friendly
- Fit within one page for freshers

---

# Resume Structure

1. Header
2. Professional Summary
3. Technical Skills
4. Projects
5. Education
6. Certifications
7. Languages
